Plumbing Problems We Fix Across Plant City

These are the calls that fill our schedule week after week in Plant City. If yours is on this list, we have fixed hundreds just like it.

  • Water heater fails and you have no hot water before a holiday

    We test the element, thermostat and dip tube first, because half of failed water heaters just need a $40 part. If the tank itself is gone, we swap in a Rheem or Bradford White unit sized for your household, usually the same day.

  • Sewer line backs up every time it rains hard

    That pattern usually means root intrusion or a cracked pipe. We run a camera down the line, show you the footage, then clear it with a hydro jetting machine and quote a permanent repair if the pipe is damaged.

  • Water bill jumped and you cannot find the leak

    A slab leak under a Plant City home can run for weeks before it surfaces. We use leak detection equipment and pressure isolation to pinpoint it, then repair with minimal concrete cutting instead of tearing up a whole room.

  • Low water pressure in one bathroom or the whole house

    We check the pressure gauge at the main, inspect for corroded galvanized pipe or a failing PRV, and test fixtures for scale buildup. Most pressure problems trace back to one of three causes, and we fix the right one.
