Privacy Policy

How QuickFlow Plumbing handles your personal information

QuickFlow Plumbing

Privacy and data

Information We Collect

When you contact us, we collect the details we need to help you: your name, phone number, email address, and the service address where the plumbing work is needed. If you describe a problem, such as a burst pipe or a clogged drain, we keep those notes with your request.

If you pay by card, our payment processor handles your card details. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.

Like most websites, our site automatically records basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. This helps us keep the site running smoothly and understand which services people are looking for.

How We Use Information

We use your information to schedule your appointment, send a technician to the right address, and follow up on the work we performed. If you ask for a quote, we use your details to prepare and share it.

We may also use your contact information to send appointment reminders, service updates, or occasional offers. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP to a text or calling our office.

We use website data to improve our pages, measure which services people search for most, and make it easier for Plant City customers to reach us.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tools to remember your preferences and keep your session working properly. These small files also help us see how visitors move through the site so we can fix confusing pages.

You can set your browser to block or delete cookies. If you do, some parts of the site may not work as smoothly, but you can still call us at 813-548-5096 to book service.

Analytics and Third-Party Tools

We use trusted third-party tools, such as website analytics and scheduling software, to understand site traffic and manage appointments. These providers see only the information needed to perform their job, and they are required to protect it.

We may also use advertising platforms to show our services to people in and around Plant City. These platforms may use cookies to display relevant ads. You can control ad personalization through your browser or device settings.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers who help us run the business, such as our scheduling platform, payment processor, and email or text messaging tools.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, if we need to protect the safety of our customers or team, or if we are involved in a business transfer. In every case, we share only what is necessary.

Data Retention

We keep your service records, including your contact details and job history, for as long as we need them to provide warranty support, answer questions about past work, and meet our legal and tax obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or remove the details that identify you.

Data Security

We protect your information with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Our team members access customer data only when they need it to do their jobs.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work hard to keep your information safe and review our practices regularly.

Your Rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also opt out of marketing messages at any time.

To make a request, call us at 813-548-5096 or send an email to the address below. We will respond within a reasonable time and let you know if we need anything else from you.

If you live in a state with additional privacy rights, such as California or Virginia, we will honor those rights as required by law.

Children's Privacy

Our services are for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, call us at 813-548-5096 and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.

If we make a significant change that affects how we use your information, we will make that clear on our website or contact you directly.
