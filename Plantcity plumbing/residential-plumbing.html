Tools and methods we use

  • RIDGID drain snake

    Clears simple clogs in kitchen, bathroom, and main drain lines. We run it through the cleanout to break up blockages without damaging the pipe.

  • Hydro jetting machine

    Blasts grease, scale, and root intrusion out of sewer lines with high-pressure water. Used when a snake isn't enough to clear the line.

  • Leak detection equipment

    Pinpoints slab leaks and hidden pipe leaks without tearing up floors or walls. Saves you money on unnecessary demolition.

  • Pipe wrench and pipe cutter

    Standard hand tools for cutting and fitting copper pipe, PEX tubing, and PVC pipe. Every truck carries a full set.

  • Soldering torch and SharkBite fittings

    Soldering torch for copper pipe joints. SharkBite push-fit fittings for fast repairs where soldering isn't practical or safe.

  • Pressure gauge

    Tests water pressure at the main line and at individual fixtures. Helps diagnose low water pressure and check for hidden leaks.

  • Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) compliance

    Every repair and installation follows the Uniform Plumbing Code and Florida Building Code. That means proper pipe sizing, correct venting, and code-compliant materials. Your work passes inspection and your insurance stays valid.

  • Pressure testing

    After any repair or installation, we pressure-test the line before closing up the wall or floor. If there's a leak, we find it before you do. No callbacks, no water damage.

What homeowners should know about plumbing repairs

Most plumbing problems start small and get expensive fast. Knowing what to watch for helps you call us before a drip becomes a flood.

  • Water pressure

    Pressure above 80 psi stresses every pipe and fixture in the house. A failing regulator is often the cause. We test it with a gauge at the main line.

  • Water heater age

    Tanks last 8 to 12 years in Plant City's hard water. After year 10, replacement usually costs less than repeated repairs. Rusty water means the tank is done.

  • Drain warning signs

    One slow drain is a local clog. Every drain in the house draining slow points to the main line. That's when a camera inspection saves you money.
