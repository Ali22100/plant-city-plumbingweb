What happens when you call us

Five steps, no mystery. Here is exactly how an emergency plumbing call runs from the moment you dial to the moment we pull out of your driveway.

  1. Phone triage

    You reach a live person, not a machine. We ask what is leaking, where, and whether the water is still running. If you do not know where your main shut-off is, we walk you to it while the truck is rolling. Two minutes on the phone often saves two hours of cleanup.

  2. Dispatch and arrival

    We send the nearest available technician and text you the arrival window. Most Plant City addresses see us in 60 to 90 minutes. If traffic on I-4 backs up, you get a heads-up rather than silence.

  3. Diagnosis on site

    We inspect the fixture, check pressure with a pressure gauge, and trace the source. A running toilet is a five-minute fix. A slab leak takes leak detection equipment and 30 to 45 minutes to pinpoint before we cut anything.

  4. Repair and test

    We make the repair with the right material for the line, whether that is copper pipe, PEX tubing or PVC. Then we pressure-test, run the fixture, and watch for drips. Nothing gets buttoned up until it holds.

  5. Cleanup and walkthrough

    We wipe down the work area, haul off old parts, and show you what failed and why. You get a plain-English rundown of what to watch for and what maintenance keeps it from happening again.

Tools and methods we use

  • Hydro jetting machine

    Blasts grease, scale and root intrusion out of sewer laterals with high-pressure water. It clears what a drain snake cannot reach and leaves the pipe wall cleaner.

  • Leak detection equipment

    Pinpoints slab leaks and hidden supply line breaks without tearing up flooring. We use it to mark the exact spot before any concrete is opened.

  • RIDGID drain snake

    Clears branch-line and main-line blockages in sinks, tubs and toilets. It is the first tool we reach for on most backed-up drain calls.

  • Pressure gauge

    Measures incoming water pressure to confirm whether a leak, running toilet or fixture failure traces back to high street pressure.

  • Pipe cutter and soldering torch

    Cuts and joins copper pipe cleanly. We use them on supply line repairs where copper matches the existing plumbing.

  • SharkBite fittings

    Push-to-connect fittings that let us make a fast, reliable repair on copper, PEX or CPVC without waiting on a solder joint to cool.

  • Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) compliance

    Every repair is sized, vented and supported to UPC standards. That means the right pipe diameter, proper slope on drain lines, and correct venting so the fix does not create a new problem downstream.

  • Florida Building Code permitting

    Water heater replacements, sewer line work and major supply line repairs get permitted where the code requires it. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection so your records stay clean.
